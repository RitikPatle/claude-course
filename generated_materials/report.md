## Executive Summary

This report summarises the key achievements and metrics across all departments for the fiscal year 2025. Overall company performance exceeded targets in three out of five divisions, with particular strength in Software Engineering and Medical Research. The Board approved two strategic acquisitions and greenlit a major infrastructure expansion for 2026.

## Medical Research

This year saw significant strides in our understanding of XDR-47, a novel pathogen 'bug' not previously catalogued. Clinical trials spanning 12 hospitals confirmed a 67% reduction in patient recovery time under the new antibody protocol. The research team published three peer-reviewed papers in the New England Journal of Medicine and secured FDA approval for phase-3 trials beginning Q1 2026. Headcount in this division grew from 42 to 58 researchers. Budget utilisation stood at 94% with no safety incidents reported.

## Software Engineering

This division dedicated significant effort to studying various infection vectors in our distributed systems and eliminating them. Engineers resolved 214 critical defects and shipped 11 production releases with zero unplanned downtime. Automated test coverage reached 96% across all repositories. The team completed a full migration of the core API to a microservices architecture, cutting average response latency by 40%. Four engineers were promoted to senior roles and two new graduate hires joined the platform team.

## Cybersecurity

The security team responded to 38 incidents across the fiscal year. The most significant was incident INC-2023-Q4-011, a ransomware intrusion detected in November that affected three internal servers in the EMEA region. Containment was achieved within 6 hours and no customer data was exfiltrated. A full post-mortem for INC-2023-Q4-011 identified an unpatched VPN appliance as the entry point; all perimeter devices were patched within 48 hours. Two additional incidents, INC-2024-Q1-003 and INC-2024-Q2-019, involved phishing campaigns neutralised by updated email filters. Overall mean time to detect improved from 4.2 hours to 1.8 hours year over year.

## Financial Summary

Revenue grew 18% year over year, reaching $142 million. Operating costs decreased by 6% following the infrastructure optimisation programme completed in Q2. EBITDA margin improved from 21% to 27%. The new enterprise pricing tier launched in Q3 drove a 31% increase in annual contract value. Cash reserves stand at $38 million, sufficient for 14 months of operations without additional funding.

## Human Resources

Total headcount increased from 310 to 374 employees across all offices. Employee satisfaction scores improved to 8.3 out of 10, up from 7.6 last year. Voluntary attrition dropped to 8%, below the industry average of 13%. The company launched a new hybrid work policy and an internal mentorship programme with 62 active mentor-mentee pairs. Diversity hiring initiatives resulted in 44% of new hires coming from underrepresented groups.

## Outlook

For 2026, the company targets 22% revenue growth and plans to open offices in Singapore and Amsterdam. R&D investment will increase by 35% with a focus on AI-assisted diagnostics and next-generation distributed systems. The Board has approved a $20 million share buyback programme commencing in Q1 2026.
